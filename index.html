REV. EDWIN EDEMIOBIAHUIHEOMA OKPARA

Apostle • Prophet • Evangelist • Pastor • Teacher

⚡ Turning Point & Conversion

Born physically in 1968 in Agbowerre of Uhude Ngodo Isuochi to Elemedum and Uba, Rev. Edwin Okpara’s trajectory changed forever on May 22, 1986 in Jos, Plateau State at the age of 18.

"On the day the enemy projected from the pit of hell to make him run mad while smoking weed, he met Jesus Christ, surrendered his life totally, and was dramatically arrested by God."

Rapid growth and maturity followed this complete surrender. Just three years later, he received the clear divine calling into full-time pastoral ministry.

❤️ Family & Marriage

At age 24, Rev. Okpara married Pst. Mrs. Mercy Ojiugo (nee Ukagha). They have remained lovebirds for nearly three decades, blessed with godly children and three lovely grandchildren.

🏛️ Leadership & Pastoral Journey

In 1990, he entered Assemblies of God Divinity School, Old Umuahia, graduating with Diplomas in Theology and American Sign Language. He later earned his BA and MA in Missions and Communication from Evangel Theological Seminary (ETS), Jos.

Ordained in 2001, he was appointed an Administrative Unit Head in 2004 at age 36—making him the youngest Unit Head under General Superintendent Rev. Dr. Charles O. Osueke.

His 17+ years in national leadership across multiple General Superintendents includes:

  • Current Role: President of New Life Pentecostal Assembly, Togo (AG Nigeria in Diaspora) & Lead Pastor of Togo-2000, Lomé.
  • National Prayer Coordinator: AG Nigeria (5+ years).
  • Ecumenical Leadership: Served 6 years as Zamfara State CAN and PFN Chairman, leading the first Federal Contingent from Zamfara to Israel (2009/2010).
  • Editorial Board: Member of Evangel Daily Meal devotional board.

🔥 Fivefold Ministry Expressions

Rev. Okpara reflects the full fivefold ministry expression to the global Church:

  • Apostolic Missionary: Pioneer missionary to Wushishi (Niger State), Mopa (Kogi State), Illela (Sokoto State), and foreign missionary to Republic of Niger.
  • Evangelist & Revivalist: Convener of Great Miracle Basket (GMB) in Isuochi, bringing monthly healings and deliverances for 6 consecutive years. Led citywide crusades across Nigeria and Equatorial Guinea.
  • Teacher & Author: Erudite scholar, ETS lecturer, and prolific author of bestsellers including Stubborn Faith for Stubborn Situations and Captured but Not Defeated (over 20,000 copies sold each).
